Who we are

One with all who belong to Christ

As brothers and sisters centered on Christ and the Bible, we stand on the ground that all regenerated Christians are one. For this reason, we take no other name than the city in which we gather.

What we believe

The gospel in a few sentences

The Bible is the inspired word of God. God is triune; He is one, yet at the same time, the Father, the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

God was incarnated, becoming a man, Jesus Christ. He died and shed His blood to accomplish redemption for our sins, and resurrected as the life-giving Spirit to regenerate us with the life of God.

He is enthroned in the Heavens and will physically come back a second time when God brings this age of grace to a close to bring in His kingdom.

Our church life

Fellowship, every day of the week

Fellowship during the week

We hold to the practice of a church-life that the New Testament saints and apostles enjoyed, which is receiving one another's portion of grace in Christ every day of the week through multiple settings.

Home gatherings

To this purpose, we gather as often as we can in the homes to worship, minister to one another, and build each other up in the faith.

Sunday service

We view Sunday as a precious and meaningful day for gathering together while enjoying our Christian life and a full church life!

Our Sunday gatherings are made up of several parts: (1) enjoying the Lord's Table, which is the practice of Christians taking the bread and the cup together to symbolize the New Covenant and remember the Lord's death until He comes; during this time, we sing hymns, pray, and mutually encourage one another in the Spirit; (2) we read through portions of scripture together and have fellowship concerning God's Word, striving to know God's will through His Word, which is our unique standard as believers.
